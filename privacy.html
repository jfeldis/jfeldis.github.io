Privacy Notice — home-auto

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Scope

home-auto is a personal, private home-automation tool operated by the owner of the home it controls. It is not a public or commercial service and no third parties use it or this website.

Data

  • No accounts, no sign-up. This website is static and collects no information of any kind.
  • OAuth flow. Authorization for the tool happens directly between your Google Account and Google's servers using the standard OAuth 2.0 flow. home-auto never sees your Google password.
  • Device data. Home state (temperature, humidity, thermostat mode) is fetched only by the owner, stored locally on the owner's own machine, and never shared, sold, or transmitted anywhere other than the Google Smart Device Management API.
