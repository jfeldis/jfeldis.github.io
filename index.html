h home-auto

What this is: home-auto is a personal smart-home automation project. It reads state and issues simple commands (e.g. thermostat setpoints) for Google Nest devices in the owner's home, using the official Google Smart Device Management API.

Who runs it: A private home-automation setup operated by the account owner. It is not offered to the public, is not a commercial service, and is not distributed as an app.

Data handling: home-auto does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data beyond the OAuth flow with Google itself. Device state is only fetched by the owner to operate their own home. See the privacy notice.
